How ratings work, privacy, and limits

Alignment: a custom review scale

1 Not aligned · 2 Slightly aligned · 3 Moderately aligned · 4 Mostly aligned · 5 Fully aligned. Missing evidence stays “Not yet reviewed.” Each rating is a suggestion until faculty review it.

This tool does not implement the proprietary Quality Matters Rubric or represent a formal Quality Matters review.

AI vulnerability: a provisional estimate

The ring is informed by the five ARMS risk categories. A fuller ring means greater resilience, not a measured probability. Delivery, supervision, and process evidence matter. Official ARMS descriptions have not been verified, so these are not official ARMS scores.

AI permission is your decision

The AI Assessment Scale (AIAS) badge uses the original 2024 level names. It records what the instructor permits. The tool never infers permission from vulnerability.

Your materials and limitations

No submitted files or reviews are saved in this app’s server storage. The AI provider processes the confirmed text under its own data policies. Browser work is lost when the tab closes unless you save a file. Clear the workspace when using a shared computer.

Text extraction can omit images, tables, numbering, or formatting. Scanned PDFs require pasted text. Linked content is not fetched. Generated ratings may still be wrong even when a quotation matches.

ARMS: De Vita and Brown · AI Assessment Scale: Perkins, Furze, Roe, and MacVaugh

PDF extraction uses Mozilla PDF.js 6.3.289, loaded from jsDelivr. DOCX extraction reads the main document text only.
