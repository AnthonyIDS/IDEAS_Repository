Module Overview and To Do List

Orient students to the topic, frame the learning, and provide a clear sequence of activities.

Make this template your own
  1. Copy the HTML and paste it into your Canvas page’s HTML editor.
  2. Replace every bracketed prompt with student-facing content.
  3. Use the hook to spark interest, the overview to orient students, and essential questions to guide inquiry. Organize the checklist in the actual learning sequence; add or remove activities to fit your module.
  4. Link exact course-item titles and map each activity to the objectives it actually supports. Define LO as Learning Objective on first use. Include due dates or link to the course schedule; check links and consistency with activity instructions before publishing.

Assignment

Make the purpose, task, and criteria clear so students understand what to do, why it matters, and what successful work looks like.

Design a clear, aligned assignment
  1. Start with the learning objectives and the evidence students need to produce. Choose a meaningful task that allows them to demonstrate that learning.
  2. Replace every bracketed prompt with student-facing content. Adapt the five suggested steps to your task; combine, reorder, or remove steps that do not apply.
  3. Use the overview for the scope, the directions for the process, and grading criteria for the qualities of successful work. Keep requirements consistent across all three.
  4. Add real resource links and a usable rubric or scoring guide. Check file access, submission settings, points, dates, and any tool or collaboration expectations before publishing.
