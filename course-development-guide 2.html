Build your course. Make it yours.

Use this guide to personalize the Begin Here and Module 1 templates in your Canvas course. Work through one page at a time.

  1. Choose a module and page. Select Begin Here! or Module 1 in the menu, then choose the page you are building.
  2. Read the Faculty Developer Notes. Open each section to see what to add to the matching section in your Canvas template.
  3. Add your course content. Follow the instructions provided. Where an IgniteAI prompt is available, you may copy it into IgniteAI to help draft your text. Replace placeholders with your own details.
  4. Review and save in Canvas. Check accuracy, links, accessibility, and your course requirements before publishing. Leave sections marked “Content already provided — no edits needed” as they are.
Review before publishing.AI prompts are optional. Review and revise any draft so it is accurate and sounds like you before adding it to Canvas.
