How to format your text (open this the first time)

Title and headings

  • Choose a template style and select Apply. The first line becomes the blue title banner.
  • Section names like Module Overview, To Do List, Purpose, Directions, Resource 1: …, Step 1: …, or Connect to Practice on their own line become styled headings.
  • Any other short line written in Title Case also becomes a heading.
  • Force a heading with ## Section or a smaller one with ### Sub-heading.

Paragraphs and lists

  • Each line is its own paragraph.
  • Start a line with -, *, or • for bullets, or 1. for numbers. Indent two spaces to nest.
  • A short label ending in a colon, like Read: or Final product:, is made bold.
  • Lines starting Tip:, Strategy for Success:, or Remember: become call-out boxes.

Links, video, and emphasis

  • Web addresses become links that open in a new tab. Use [Read: Article Title](https://…) for descriptive link text.
  • A YouTube link alone on a line becomes a responsive video with a Watch link below it.
  • **bold** and *italic* work. --- adds a divider.
  • Pasting from Word or Google Docs keeps headings, bold, lists, and links.

Snippets from the HTML Code library

  • Highlight text in the Preview, pick a snippet category and design, and select Apply. The snippet wraps or restyles the whole paragraphs, lists, and headings you touched.
  • Accordions, dropdowns, and Clickable Steps make one item per heading you highlight.
  • Quick Check uses the first line as the question and the list below it as answers. End the right answer with *.
  • Snippets stay applied while you keep editing your text. Use Undo or Remove all to take them off.
  • You can also click into the Preview and fix wording there, or edit the code itself on the HTML tab next to Your text. After that, the page keeps your edits. Select Rebuild from Your text to go back to building it from your text.

You can edit this HTML directly; the Preview updates as you type. In Canvas, open the page editor, switch to the HTML editor (</>), and paste.
