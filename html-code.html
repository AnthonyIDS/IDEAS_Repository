Answer: What is a Large Language Model?

An LLM focuses on understanding and generating text. LLMs are the engine underneath the hood of chatbots like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini.

LLMs are trained on enormous amounts of text (books, websites, code, and articles). The model learns to predict the next word in a sentence, billions of times, until it builds a rich statistical map of human language.

Think of it less like a database and more like a very well-read collaborator who learned everything by reading and can now converse, write, predict, and create. The key insight is that the model does not look up answers. It generates them, which is both its power and its weakness.

Good Example:

In the medical field, an individual choosing to become a neonatal nurse should be aware of education requirements, typical job duties, the potential salary and future employment prospects.

A student looking into becoming a network security specialist should know the required education, expected salary, employment outlook, and the primary responsibilities of the profession.

Bad Example:

There are many reasons why I want to be a neonatal nurse.

1.) Vague. "There are many reasons why" does not tell the reader what will actually be discussed.
2.) Uses first person ("I"), which is not permitted for this assignment.
3.) The reader has no clear direction about the main points of the paragraph.
