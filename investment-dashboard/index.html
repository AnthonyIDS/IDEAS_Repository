How the score works

The score is a simple 0–100 policy checklist, not a forecast or statistical risk model. Eight equally weighted checks: 10–15 holdings, 60–70% core, 5–8 stocks, direct stocks ≤8%, estimated equity sectors ≤25%, at least six direct-stock sectors, international exposure, and bond exposure. ETF security overlap, correlations and volatility are not measured. Five percentage points of drift triggers review, not a trade.
